# Comparison scoring v0.1.0 (experimental)

This is a product rubric, not Sesheta's formula, a classical standard, or a validated probability. Do not describe it as a percentage of romantic success.

Each direction evaluates the source chart against the receiver's existing strength-engine assessment. The receiver's primary useful element is assigned 90, supportive element 75, caution elements 25, other elements 50. These are editorial constants, not measured probabilities. Special structures without a primary useful element withhold that direction and the overall score; borderline assessments receive a note.

- 40% elemental supply: weighted mean of suitability across source visible stems (2 each, excluding the day stem) and hidden stems (1, 0.6, 0.35). The day stem is excluded to avoid counting it again in the final component. This uses presence, not a claim of full seasonal qi measurement.
- 40% positional interaction: same-position branch checks only, six harmonies 80, six clashes 25, otherwise 50. No claim that a combination transforms. Position weights day/month/year/hour: love 5/2/1/2, family 4/3/2/3, work 2/5/2/3, friendship 3/2/5/1, overview 3/3/3/3. Missing positions are excluded from numerator and denominator. Neutral means no recognized relation in this limited model, not proof of compatibility.
- 20% source day-master element suitability for the receiver. Ten-god polarity is NOT separately scored in v1: avoid pretending a label alone measures behavior or double-counting the same stem relationship.

Round each directional weighted total to an integer, then round the mean of both directional integers for the overall score. UI component scores are rounded for display only. Total bands: >=70, >=55, >=40, <40; these are provisional editorial labels.

Unknown second birth time: remove its hour pillar and hour interactions before any assessment, rather than using the noon placeholder. No missing-time penalty. Scores are provisional: unknown time can change strength and the day boundary at 23:00; solar-term boundaries can also affect the chart. This version does not enumerate possible birth times or claim a confidence interval.

Limits: does not score cross-position interactions, punishments, harms, transformations, noblemen, timing, or actual behavior. Scores and existing narrative are distinct: the latter is still a simpler element/branch interpretation. A future narrative rewrite should derive every statement from the same evidence. Persist the version with new reports; old saved reports remain unchanged and show an explicit no-score notice.
