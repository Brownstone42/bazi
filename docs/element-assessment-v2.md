# Five-element assessment: strength/0.2.0

Bug: ordinary strong charts previously selected output and wealth, but their controlling element was silently left out of both helpful and caution lists. Calendar scoring treated that omission as neutral and hid its zero-point factor. These were two distinct defects.

Change: for regular strong/very-strong charts without an overriding climate requirement, the controller is now an additional supportive element alongside wealth. Output remains primary. All five elements now carry explicit relation, status and explanation metadata. Ordinary weak charts keep their controller in the caution set; balanced charts do not receive this extra controller bonus. Potential follow/dominant structures remain unresolved. Existing climate-priority rules remain in force. A missing element alone never earns a bonus.

This is still a bounded strength-balance heuristic, NOT complete Useful God determination, a seasonal quality/tier model, or proof that the controller is always auspicious. It does not infer Sesheta S-tier. It does not resolve the condition of a newly arriving controlling element, stem combinations or transformations, or every climate need. These require separate rules and validation before stronger claims.

The default 26 Aug 1989 11:30 Bangkok chart remains regular strong Earth. Wood now has supportive status (Power relation); Metal remains primary and Water supportive. Calendar gives Wood +1 raw (+8 on the display scale) as the main daily element, +0.7 raw as the branch element. Other factors can still make a Wood day low-scoring. Comparison gives additional supportive elements the existing 75 rubric value; its version is bumped to compatibility/0.2.0. Natal-life-area and luck-cycle helpful-element checks share the expanded list. No stored reports, quotas, calendar pillar calculation or birth inputs are rewritten.

Both calendar elemental factors are always displayed, including zero. Detailed reasons come from the explicit five-element assessment. Tests cover the five controlling pairs, strong versus weak versus balanced versus special patterns, climate priority, and Wood day-stems on Sep/Oct 7,8,17,18,27,28 in 2026.
